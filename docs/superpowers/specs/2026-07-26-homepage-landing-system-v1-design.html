The short version

The homepage gets one measurable job: turn anonymous visitors into people we can reach, by letting them save a stay or share it with the person they book with — by text or email. No accounts, one field, one tap. Every release now passes a live staging site before it can touch reset.club, and every Monday we read the numbers and ship one improvement.

Why capture, not conversionA 3-night stay is a considered purchase. The homepage starts relationships.

72% of traffic is direct — people who already know the brand. Almost nobody books a $1,500 stay on first visit. The win is capturing a phone number or email so the drops engine and Klaviyo can work toward the right window. Booking intent stays protected as the co-metric: the ready-now path must never get slower.

Desktop is the leak: 60% of visitors, 70% bounce. Mobile bounces at just 31%. “Text it to your phone” turns the worst surface into a feeder for the best one.
Save & Share, by text or emailOne field. No login. Captures both halves of the booking decision.

Every drop row and property section gets SAVE and SHARE. Save asks for a phone or email and sends back a link that reopens the site with that stay pinned. Share adds a “send to” field — because every booking has two people in it, the one who falls in love and the one who approves. On phones, the native share sheet does the work.

Returning through a saved link is the first real personalization: your stay, pinned at the top, live price, one button. If the window closed, the next comparable one appears instead.

Notifications follow the honest-urgency rule: a confirmation, and one window-closing reminder. Nothing manufactured, no discounts.

Trust on the homepage2,000+ five-star reviews exist. The homepage shows none of them.

A proof strip lands near the hero in the existing chip style: 2,000+ FIVE-STAR REVIEWS · VOGUE · DWELL · NYT. Also this cycle: figure out why email signups tracked zero all week (broken event or broken flow), and watch ten desktop session replays before deciding any visual redesign.

Staging before productionNo code reaches reset.club without passing a live staging site.

A permanent staging URL (Cloudflare Pages branch deploy) gets every change first. The deploy script runs the behavioral smoke test — the one that catches dead links and broken lanes — against staging, and refuses to ship to production until the same commit passes. Staging never sends real texts or emails and its test data stays out of the real lists.

The weekly loopEvery Monday: read the funnel, watch replays, ship one swing.

New events track the whole journey: sheet opened, identity captured, link opened, return visit, booking intent. A PostHog dashboard holds the funnel and the guardrails. At 163 visitors a week, A/B tests can’t reach significance — so the cadence is one meaningful, measured change per week, annotated in PostHog so next Monday’s read is honest.
